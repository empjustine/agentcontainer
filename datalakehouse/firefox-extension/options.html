slurp — browsing history and bookmarks

This extension streams your local Firefox history, bookmarks, and page titles to the loopback browsing daemon. It never reads page content, never injects a content script, and never talks to Firefox Sync.

The requirements this implements are docs/d204; the daemon end is docs/d205. The queue is bounded, so a long daemon outage drops the oldest visits and the next backfill sweep re-reads them — removals are never dropped that way.
